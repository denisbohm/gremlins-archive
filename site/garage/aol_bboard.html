Here is the information on getting to the Garage Kits, Vinyl Kits AOL Bulletin Board:
  1. Get to the "keyword" search box using the keyword menu item which is under the Go To menu.
  2. Enter exchange and hit return. The exchange area is displayed.
  3. Click on collector's corner.
  4. On the resultant list click on Scale modeling Collector's Board.
  5. On the next display, click on List Topics.
  6. On that final display you will see Garage Kits, Vinyl Kits.
